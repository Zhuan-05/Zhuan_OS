# Decision Workflow

Use this when a decision has trade-offs.

## Goal

What are we trying to achieve?

## Options

What are the realistic choices?

## Cost

What does each option require?

## Risk

What can go wrong?

## Reversibility

Can we undo it? At what cost?

## Long-Term Value

Which option improves future judgment or systems?

## Next Action

What is the smallest step that makes the decision real?
