# Thinking Protocol

Use this for important thinking, not for tiny tasks.

## Reality

What is true now? What evidence do we have?

## Structure

What are the parts, routes, constraints, and dependencies?

## Judgment

What matters most? What is noise?

## Risk

What can go wrong? What is irreversible?

## Value

What action creates the most useful progress?

## Action

What is the smallest concrete next step?

## Review

What happened? What signal should be remembered?
