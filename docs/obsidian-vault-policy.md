# Obsidian Vault Policy

Zhuan OS may coexist with Obsidian files, but agent work must respect vault boundaries.

## Protected Areas

- Do not touch `D:\Zhuan_Vault`.
- Do not modify Obsidian vault files unless Zhuan explicitly asks for Obsidian work.
- Do not modify `.obsidian/` settings unless explicitly approved.
- Do not move, delete, rename, or bulk rewrite notes without confirmation.

## Allowed By Default

- Create repo framework files requested by Zhuan.
- Read non-secret repo files needed to understand the task.
- Add new docs, prompts, and logs under explicit repo framework paths.

## When In Doubt

Ask before editing anything that looks like personal notes, vault configuration, archived material, or synced knowledge-base content.
